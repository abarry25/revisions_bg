Pre-beta revision plan

Read this for the reasoning behind the sequence. For day-to-day tracking, use the interactive checklist instead.

🐻 Send to beta readers: September 30, 2026

The developmental architecture works — Maeren's arc is causal, the sister conflict is earned, Rían's betrayal changes the central relationship, and the climax grows from the novel's established conflict between suppression, listening, control, and force. What stands between this draft and beta readers isn't a rewrite. It's finishing the page, then working outward from story logic to prose to hygiene, in that order — because polishing a sentence in a scene with a missing causal beat wastes the polish.

Rule for this entire period: this is no longer an open developmental rewrite. Every revision solves an identified problem. New ideas go into a Book Two / parking-lot document unless they expose a genuine continuity or comprehension issue.

Phase 1 — Lock the story

Aug 26 – Sep 2 · Developmental cleanup only — do not polish sentences yet.

Build the master revision list — Aug 26
  • Set up the working filesDuplicate the manuscript as PRE-BETA MASTER; start a separate Book Two / parking-lot doc; mark which chapters are affected by remaining developmental issues and leave the rest alone.
  • Remove drafting artifacts and finish unfinished passagesEmbedded Q&A/dev tables, every <<>> / TODO / placeholder, corrupted lines (Throne Room p.19), missing beats (Rían's surveillance ch. p.81–82, Maeren's approach to the Bear p.84, market/village transition p.104), stray brackets and editorial comments.
  • Confirm these decisions remain settled unless the manuscript itself reveals a contradictionMaeren stays quiet/investigative, no added combat competence · Maeren/Brynna don't need Book One reconciliation · Rían doesn't need full redemption · the ending stays unresolved · the Bear transformation doesn't need to be triumphant · Order and Elders stay ideological mirrors · magic/listening/control relationship stays implicit · Riven/hydrangea ending stays · no pull toward romantasy convention.
Maeren causality pass — Aug 27–28
  • Read Maeren's chapters consecutively; verify want → question → knowledge → choice → consequence for eachBear investigation stays curiosity-driven · archive/prophecy research has a visible evidence chain · capture reads as strategic compliance once Brynna is threatened · Astralis compliance evolves · she keeps observing Rían and Cordova · revelations connect to questions she's already carrying · Gwyrionez's info revises her theories · she connects the betrayal evidence herself · the journal functions as evidence, not exposition · her final decision clearly originates in her. End-of-pass test: complete "Because Maeren ___, ___ happens" for every chapter.
Astralis pass — Aug 29–30
  • Track the investigation, captivity/control, and Rían engines simultaneouslyDoes each revelation answer or complicate a question Maeren already has? Does her compliance have a clear reason? Does trust in Rían rise for a specific, incremental reason each time, and does the later surveillance reveal specifically contaminate earlier intimacy?
  • Audit training scenes for redundancyFor each: what's different after this scene? Compress only scenes doing genuinely the same job — don't cut a quiet scene just because it lacks external action.
Sister & emotional continuity pass — Aug 31
  • Track wound → response → suppression → resurfacing → changed behavior across Maeren/Brynna, Eamon, Nyara, their mother, and RivenEamon keeps affecting both sisters · Brynna's accusation stays consequential · Maeren's understanding of her mother changes · Nyara's death stays present · Brynna carries guilt after the separation · Riven/hydrangea payoff stays intact. Do not manufacture reconciliation.
Rían relationship pass — Sep 1
  • Track threat → captor → uncertainty → curiosity → recognition → safety → attraction → trust → warning → betrayalConfirm each stage-shift has a specific cause. Do not redeem him further — accountability is Book Two's job.
Climax pass — Sep 2
  • Confirm Maeren's choice, the unchosen consequence, and Rían's choice stay distinct at the VeilMaeren deliberately forcing her power open is her authored choice. The power overwhelming her is a consequence she can't predict. Rían's moonstone removal is his own separate decision (see Fix Before Beta above).
  • Clarify the portal confrontation / final transformation proseHardest sequence to visualize — imprecise verbs and stacked metaphors (fire/ice/stone/blood) arrive faster than cause-and-effect. Highest-value clarity fix in the book.
  • Tighten spatial clarity in the Bear's last standMany simultaneous combatants blur positioning; be selective with figurative language here specifically.

Lock: developmental lock after Sep 2. No new structural revision unless you discover a genuine contradiction.

Phase 2 — Prose pass

Sep 3 – 12 · The story is locked. Stop thinking about plot.

Sentence-level clarity — do first
  • Fix recurring sentence-level clarity issues throughoutDangling/misattached modifiers, ambiguous pronouns, anatomically unclear language ("shuttered"→"shuddered," "gagged"), abstract verbs standing in for simple images ("space to breach," "shadow compiled"), agreement errors.
Somatic language — Sep 3–5
  • Audit heart / chest / stomach / gut / breath / throat / blood / heat / cold / tightening / burning / trembling / tearsKeep strong bodily writing — remove or vary only the repetitions that have become default shorthand. Rough POV goals: Maeren = perception/environment, Brynna = movement/readiness, Aurelian = shame/recoil, Rían = restraint/suppressed reaction breaking through.
Interiority & rhetorical questions — Sep 6–7
  • Audit stacked internal questions ("she wondered," "what if," "why")If three questions communicate one uncertainty, keep the strongest. Goal isn't fewer questions — it's eliminating ones that restate the one before.
Emotional redundancy — Sep 8–9
  • Cut redundant channels wherever emotion shown → bodily reaction → metaphor → emotion named → rhetorical restatement all stack togetherEspecially: Eamon, Nyara, the sister rupture, the capture, the rain, Rían intimacy, the betrayal, the transformation, Riven. Goal is hierarchy, not minimalism.
Gesture audit — Sep 10
  • Audit recurring gestures: smirk, brow furrow, head tilt, crossed arms, clenched jaw/fist, sigh, shrug, eyes widen/narrow, swallowing, noddingKeep character-specific gestures; change only the repetitions flattening characters together.
POV differentiation — Sep 11
  • Read 5–10 pages from each POV consecutively; check if you could identify the narrator with names removedFocus especially on Maeren vs. Rían, the closest pair per the editorial report.
Lyricism protection — Sep 12
  • Reread the hydrangea garden, forest, dreams, Astralis landscape, rain, both transformations, and the Riven ending — restore anything cut merely for being descriptiveAn entire pass whose job is partly not to cut things.

Lock: prose lock after Sep 12.

Phase 3 — Continuity & manuscript hygiene

Sep 13 – 19 · No developmental editing.

Names & terminology — Sep 13–14
  • Build a canonical spelling sheet and fix every name/term variant globallyMaeren, Brynna, Aurelian, Rían, Orlíen, Cydar, Nyara, Vaela, Gwyrionez, Minvaera/Maevira, Astrean, Sha'thi, Nymari, Velmora, Astralis, Eos, Talauvmor, Syrithien, Veil terminology — plus Godric Moore and "Order of Divinity" from the Fix Before Beta list.
Object continuity — Sep 15
  • Track where each object begins, who has it, and where it endsMoonstone, Rían's journal, the prophecy/book, weapons, Riven, horses/lairs, the hydrangea charm, maps, injuries.
Knowledge continuity — Sep 16
  • For every major revelation, confirm who knows it, when they learned it, and howNyara = Bear, Maeren's power, the lineage, the prophecy, Rían's surveillance, the Elders' motives, Order activity, Veil mechanics, family history.
Timeline & geography — Sep 17
  • Check days/nights, travel duration, healing time, and parallel timelines against each otherIncluding the journal date and hunt day-count issues from the Fix Before Beta list. Doesn't need to be beautiful, needs to work.
Magic continuity — Sep 18
  • Build a private ability/mechanism/cost/limits reference sheet and verify the manuscript against itExtraction, healing, environmental manipulation, the shadow creature, the Veils, Bear transformation, the moonstone, magic's cost, Maeren's sensory abilities.
Final manuscript hygiene sweep — Sep 19
  • Final search for any remaining artifacts or formatting breaks<<, >>, TODO, Q&A tables, placeholder text, duplicated paragraphs, corrupted characters, inconsistent chapter headings, missing scene breaks. The one hard prerequisite before outside readers see the manuscript.

Phase 4 — The reader pass

Sep 20 – 25 · Stop editing while you read.

Read like a reader — Sep 20–24
  • Export to a format that feels different from your working doc; read ~20% per day, marking onlyC = confused, B = bored, R = repetition, E = emotional beat didn't land, CONT = continuity issue, 🔥 = this works, don't touch it. No editing while reading.
Triage your marks — Sep 25
  • Fix genuine confusion, continuity errors, obvious repetition, and broken transitions onlyDo NOT fix "maybe readers won't like this" or "what if the second act..." — those go on the beta-question list, not into the manuscript.

Phase 5 — Beta package

Sep 26 – 29

Final formatting — Sep 26
  • Clean formatting passTitle page, consistent chapter headings and scene breaks, readable font/spacing, page numbers, remove comments/track changes, verify italics and accented characters.
Beta questions — Sep 27
  • Draft ~12–15 beta questions without revealing your intended answersMaeren (want, change, unclear choices) · Astralis (pacing, cohesion with Velmora, information load) · Sisters (understanding of the conflict, feelings about the unresolved ending) · Rían (when did trust begin, how the surveillance reveal reframes earlier scenes) · Climax (what was each character choosing) · Ending (what pulls you into Book Two).
Beta instructions — Sep 28
  • Write reader instructions: what to mark, and what NOT to doThey don't need to line-edit unless something repeatedly distracts them. Don't tell them what you're worried about before they read.
Freeze — Sep 29
  • Lock the beta fileCreate the final beta file, back up the working manuscript separately, back up the beta version, label it clearly, prepare the recipient list and instructions. No more editing after this.

Guardrails

Ask beta readers — don't preemptively fix

  • Does Astralis feel too long?
  • Do the two realms feel disconnected while the characters are separated?
  • Does Maeren feel agentic?
  • Does the unresolved Maeren/Brynna relationship feel intentional?
  • Does Rían earn the betrayal, or does it feel unearned?
  • Does Rían stay morally complicated rather than accidentally reading as redeemed?
  • Is Aurelian sympathetic enough to follow?
  • Is the lore comprehensible?
  • Does the climax read as intentional complexity rather than confusion?
  • Does the ending create Book Two desire?
  • Which mysteries are predictable?
  • Which emotional moments land hardest?

Do not reopen before beta

  • Core concept
  • Maeren's fundamental personality
  • The four-POV architecture
  • The Bear Guardian premise
  • The two-realm concept
  • Order/Elders thematic mirroring
  • The Maeren/Brynna philosophical conflict
  • Rían's morally compromised role
  • The unresolved Book One ending
  • Magic-as-listening/release vs. control/force theme
  • The Riven ending
  • Fundamental series architecture
